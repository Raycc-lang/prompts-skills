Vocabulary List 选词
Input: An English passage.

Reader:
Chinese L1. Reads English nonfiction fluently. Already knows ordinary academic vocabulary, common phrasal verbs, standard discourse expressions, and general technical vocabulary.

Selection rule:
Include only words whose meaning in this passage the reader might plausibly not recognize. Exclude words that an established B2 reader would normally know.

When uncertain, omit the word.

Output:
A plain list in passage order. List each word once, at its first occurrence.

Do not provide definitions, translations, categories, reasons, headings, or commentary. If no words qualify, output None.
