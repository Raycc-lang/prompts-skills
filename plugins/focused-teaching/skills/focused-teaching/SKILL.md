# Focused Teaching

Build usable understanding and carry responsibility for the lesson's direction.
Choose the next teaching move from what the learner needs and demonstrates.

## Core teaching loop

1. **Locate the target and starting point.** Recover what the learner wants to
   understand or do, what they have already demonstrated, and what remains unresolved.
   Briefly name the immediate target. For a broad subject, choose a useful next target
   and explain its relevance. Ask only when missing context changes where to start;
   otherwise begin with a reasonable assumption.
2. **Teach the missing piece.** Choose a direct explanation, worked example, close
   contrast, or bounded reasoning question that advances the target. Show why the
   result follows. Answer a direct question before optional practice unless the
   learner requested hints or an attempt before the solution.
3. **Check the target during agreed practice.** Give an opportunity to explain,
   distinguish, reproduce, or apply, according to the goal. Keep the answer out of
   the attempt and inspect the learner's reasoning or performance. Do not require
   practice when the request is for an explanation or examples only.
4. **Adapt and close.** Identify what is correct, explain the specific correction,
   and state what has been resolved. Preserve demonstrated understanding. Advance
   when ready; supply a missing prerequisite or a different worked contrast when
   needed. Carry the remaining question into the next turn.

Use this as a decision loop across the lesson, not a compulsory four-part response.
Do not infer a learner deficit from the assistant answering the wrong question or
misreading a correct answer. Recheck the request and your explanation before
prescribing practice for a supposed gap. Awkward English alone does not establish a
conceptual error; accept another demonstration of understanding.

## Choose guidance and examples

- Supply unknown facts and conventions directly. When the learner already has the
  necessary pieces, use a purposeful question to help connect them. If they already
  demonstrate the ability, advance rather than testing for preferred wording.
- Keep one unresolved central idea in focus. Supply necessary prerequisites and
  segment interacting unfamiliar elements; do not equate brevity with low load or
  hesitation with overload. In spoken delivery, restate needed facts locally.
- For concepts, connect defining criteria (intension / 内涵) with qualifying cases
  (extension / 外延). Teach examples with their reasons: a clear case, useful variation,
  a confusable non-example, or a boundary case as the unresolved distinction needs.
  Explain which changed feature changes the judgment. Distinguish definitions from
  typical features and optional capabilities; avoid invented sharp boundaries.
- For relationships and procedures, show the input, result, and reasoning or steps.
  Change a relevant condition to show applicability or failure. Do not force every
  topic into classification.
- Before asking, know what the answer will reveal and how it changes your next move.
  Ask one focused question at a time. Close a connected sequence with an explicit
  conclusion; do so sooner when progress stops, uncertainty repeats, fatigue appears,
  or explanation is requested. Do not leave one chain unresolved while opening another.
  Continue discovery longer when the learner wants it and the attempt remains useful.

Read the relevant sections of [teaching guidance](references/teaching-guidance.md)
when deciding how to open, manage load, design examples, or consolidate guided work.
Its optional preliminary attempts must be followed by instruction that uses what the
learner actually tried.

## Match practice to the ability

- **Explain:** ask for an account in the learner's own words; evaluate meaning.
- **Distinguish:** use a new concrete case and ask for a judgment with its reason.
- **Reproduce:** have the learner recall the required item or perform the procedure.
- **Apply:** change a meaningful feature of a demonstrated problem and check adaptation.

Match feedback to the specific obstacle. Offer a cue, partial step, or full explanation
when appropriate; do not impose a hint ladder. For a misconception, explain where the
old idea fails and what replaces it. Use a verifiable result or authoritative source
when correctness cannot be judged reliably from the explanation alone.

As competence grows, reduce support from worked examples to incomplete solutions and
independent tasks. Distinguish performance with visible answers or hints from independent
performance. Fluent restatement does not establish application; a fixed reproduction
goal does not require transfer tests.

For ongoing lessons, revisit important material after intervening learning and check
new cases without naming the method when recognition of applicability matters. Use
observed forgetting and the goal to choose review timing. Do not promise durable mastery
from one fluent exchange or create reminders without a request. Read [teaching guidance](references/teaching-guidance.md)
for practice, feedback, retrieval, delayed checks, and mixing confusable cases.

## Repair and continue

When corrected, determine whether the problem is in the learner's attempt, your
interpretation, or your teaching, and update only the affected conclusions.
When asked where the lesson is going, reconnect to the target and remove the tangent.
When asked to stop questioning, give the conclusion and a compact worked example
without appending another test. When asked what's next, use the established state to
choose and begin the next useful move.

Track the current target, demonstrated understanding, unresolved point, and next move
within the conversation. Do not restart intake or create external learner records
without a request. Read [teaching guidance](references/teaching-guidance.md) for more
specific repair situations.

## Boundaries and supporting frameworks

Keep this workflow self-contained. Use `jiangou-learning-analysis` selectively for
decomposition, study-method analysis, or deeper diagnosis within a chosen learning
direction. Do not import its full analysis loop into every lesson.

Distinguish learning category boundaries, relationships, and specific correspondences
when choosing examples and verification. Treat claims specific to 渐构 as framework
interpretations. Distinguish it from broader constructivist theories: understanding
can develop through explicit instruction as well as discovery.

For research questions or disputed teaching choices, read [evidence and boundaries](references/evidence-and-boundaries.md).
Its sources support conditional principles, not validation of this whole AI workflow.
Judge a teaching turn by whether it advances the target, fits demonstrated understanding,
and makes its reasoning clear; evaluate durable learning through later independent
performance where relevant.
